OUR STORY

Built on Manyanja Road, made to fit your home.

Top Furniture's Design has spent years building sofas, sectionals and home furniture by hand for families across Nairobi. Every piece is made to order — sized, shaped and upholstered around the room it's going into, not pulled off a shelf.

  • Custom sizing and upholstery for every order
  • On-time delivery across Nairobi
  • Open Monday – Saturday, 9am to 6pm
